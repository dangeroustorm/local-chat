Game Rules

Gravity 5-in-a-Row: Played on a 9x9 grid. Clicking any column drops a piece to the lowest available slot in that column.

Win Condition: Align 5 or more of your pieces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

High-Performance AI: Features Negamax search, Alpha-Beta pruning, and Zobrist Hashing running on background Web Workers.
